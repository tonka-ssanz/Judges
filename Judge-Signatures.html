Judge's Signature Form

SSANZ is offering trial organisers the option of printing judges' signatures on judging sheets, and on certificates where they can print them, to help everything run smoothly on the day of the trial. It also saves you hand-signing each one. If you're happy to take part, please fill in this form, sign three times in the boxes, and send it back.

What you're agreeing to

  • SSANZ may keep a digital copy of your signature and print it only on certificates and judging sheets for trials you judge. It won't be used on any other document.
  • It will be kept, with the other judges' signatures, in a single password-protected file in the members-only area of the SSANZ website, managed by the Judges Development Subcommittee administrator.
  • Organisations running trials will download that file to their own computers to prepare the certificates and judging sheets for trials you judge.
  • You can withdraw at any time by emailing ssanzjdsubcommittee@gmail.com. Your signature will be removed from the next version of the file.

By signing in the boxes below, you agree to the points above.

For a clean signature

  • Use a black felt-tip or gel pen, not ballpoint or blue ink.
  • Sign inside each box, not touching the edges.
  • Photograph in good daylight, phone held directly above, no shadow on the page.

Sending it back

  1. Email a photo or scan to ssanzjdsubcommittee@gmail.com, or post it or hand it to the administrator.
  2. Put your name in the subject line.
  3. Please send it to this address only. You'll get a reply when it arrives.

Your photo and this form are deleted once your signature has been prepared. Personal information is handled under the Privacy Act 2020.

Good to know

  • Nothing leaves your computer. The photo is read and cleaned in this page, and the finished file is saved straight to your Downloads folder.
  • File name is the judge's name, e.g. Sam Example.png. The trial tools will match it against the judge's name, so type it exactly as it appears in the judges list, with the same spelling and spacing.
  • Size: files are saved at about 1000 pixels wide at most. That prints cleanly on a certificate and is too small to make a convincing full-size copy.
  • Blotchy or grey background? Move the clean-up slider towards Remove more shadow. If strokes break up, move it the other way. A very dark or blurry photo can't be rescued; ask the judge for another in daylight.
  • iPhone photos (HEIC) may not open. Email the photo to yourself (it's sent as a JPG) or take a screenshot of it, then drop that in.
  • A judge withdraws? Delete their file from the signature set and issue a new version, so older copies stop being used.
